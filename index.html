I'm a Ph.D. candidate in computer vision and graphics at Simon Fraser University, advised by Prof. Ali Mahdavi-Amiri. I turn pretrained generative models into controllable tools for image editing, composition, and video segmentation, with work at SIGGRAPH, SIGGRAPH Asia, and ICLR. In summer 2026 I interned at Waabi, working on world models for driving.
